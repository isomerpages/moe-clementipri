<p><strong>Head Prefects of Clementi Primary School</strong>
</p>
<p>We honour the dedication and service of our past Head Prefects, whose
leadership and commitment have shaped the culture of student leadership
in CTPS. As the school transitioned from the Prefectorial Board to the
Student Leaders' Council in 2024, we take this opportunity to recognise
those Clementians who served with dedication and passion — their legacy
and contributions will always be remembered.</p>
