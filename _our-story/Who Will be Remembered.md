<h4><strong>Farewell to Beloved Staff</strong></h4>
<p>Clementi Primary School bids a heartfelt farewell to our cherished staff
members. Though they leave at different times, each of them has made an
indelible mark on our school community, and their contributions will always
be remembered.</p>
<p><strong>Mrs. Cheryl Lim, Vice Principal Acad</strong>
</p>
<p></p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="width: 100%" height="auto" width="100%" alt="" src="/images/2025/z3.jpg">
</div>
<p>Mrs Cheryl Lim embarks on a new journey to pursue a leadership course
at the National Institute of Education. During her tenure, Mrs Lim demonstrated
commendable leadership, compassion, and dedication. Her contributions to
our school have been invaluable, and we are deeply grateful for her service.</p>
<p></p>
<p><strong>Ms Tung Ai Jui, Vice Principal (Admin)</strong>
</p>
<p></p>
<p></p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="width: 100%" height="auto" width="100%" alt="Vice Principal Admin Ms Tung's farewell photo" src="/images/tung_farewell.jpg">
</div>
<p>After seven incredible years of service, Ms Tung Ai Jui will be continuing
her journey as Vice Principal (Admin) at another primary school. Her dedication,
passion, and care for our Clementian community will be greatly missed.
We wish her all the best in her new role and know she will continue to
make a positive impact wherever she goes.</p>
<p></p>
<p><strong>Mrs. Lim Boon Wah, Teacher</strong>
</p>
<p></p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="width: 100%" height="auto" width="100%" alt="" src="/images/2025/z4.jpg">
</div>
<p>We bid a special farewell to Mrs Lim Boon Wah, who leaves behind an extraordinary
legacy of 60 years of service to the teaching fraternity. Six decades of
teaching, guiding, and inspiring students is a truly remarkable achievement.
We are deeply grateful for the profound impact she has had on all those
she has taught.</p>
<p></p>
<p><strong>Head Prefects of Clementi Primary School</strong>
</p>
<p>We honour the dedication and service of our past Head Prefects, whose
leadership and commitment have shaped the culture of student leadership
in CTPS. As the school transitioned from the Prefectorial Board to the
Student Leaders' Council in 2024, we take this opportunity to recognise
those Clementians who served with dedication and passion — their legacy
and contributions will always be remembered.</p>
